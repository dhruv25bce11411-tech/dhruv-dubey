# 📌 Case Study: UPI Fraud

UPI fraud is a common type of cybercrime in which scammers trick users into transferring money through fake payment requests or links. It usually happens when a fraudster contacts the victim through a phone call, message, or social media, pretending to be a bank official, customer care agent, or even a known person.

The scam typically starts when the victim is asked to click on a link or scan a QR code to receive money. In reality, the QR code is used to debit money from the victim’s account. Sometimes, fraudsters also ask for OTPs or UPI PINs, claiming it is required for verification. Once the victim shares these details or approves the request, money is instantly transferred to the scammer’s account.

Students, elderly people, and individuals who are not fully aware of digital payment systems are the most common targets. The consequences include financial loss, stress, and loss of trust in digital platforms.

This case highlights the importance of awareness and caution while using online payment systems.

# 🛡️ Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

- Never share your OTP or UPI PIN with anyone  
- Do not scan QR codes to receive money  
- Always verify unknown calls or messages  
- Use official banking apps only  
- Avoid clicking on suspicious links  
- Enable two-factor authentication  
- Regularly check your bank transactions  
- Do not share personal information on social media  

## 💰 UPI Safety Tips
- Always double-check payment requests before approving  
- Do not accept money requests from unknown sources
