## 📌 Project Overview
This Digital Literacy Portfolio has been developed as part of the Digital Literacy course to understand and apply essential digital skills. The project focuses on creating awareness about safe and responsible use of digital tools, building a professional online presence, and understanding cyber security practices.

The portfolio consists of five tasks, each aligned with a specific module of the course.

---

## 🧩 Task 1: Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic
In this task, I created an infographic using Canva to explain the concept of digital literacy. The infographic highlights key topics such as safe internet practices, professional online presence, and email etiquette. This task helped me present important information in a simple and visually appealing way.

📁 Folder: `task-1-presentation/`
