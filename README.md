# 📂 Task 5: Cybercrime Awareness

## 📌 Overview
This task focuses on understanding different types of cybercrime and spreading awareness about safe online practices. It helps in recognizing common online threats and learning how to prevent them.

---

## 📄 Case Study
For this task, I created a case study on **UPI Fraud**, which is one of the most common cybercrimes in India. The case study explains what UPI fraud is, how it occurs through fake calls, links, and QR codes, and how users can be tricked into sharing sensitive information like OTPs and UPI PINs.

It also highlights the types of people who are commonly targeted, such as students and individuals who are not fully aware of digital payment systems. The consequences include financial loss and loss of trust in digital platforms.

---

## 🛡️ Prevention Checklist
A detailed prevention checklist was created to help users stay safe online. It includes important practices such as not sharing OTPs, avoiding suspicious links, verifying payment requests, and using secure applications.

Special focus was given to UPI safety, including verifying QR codes and not approving unknown payment requests.
