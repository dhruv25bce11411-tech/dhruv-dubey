# 📂 Task 1: Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic

## 📌 Overview
This folder contains my Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic created as part of Task 1 of the Digital Literacy Project. The aim of this task was to design a simple and visually engaging resource to help students understand the importance of digital literacy.

---

## 🎨 Tool Used
I used Canva to design the infographic. It is an easy-to-use online design tool that provides templates, icons, and customization options to create attractive visuals.

---

## 📊 Topics Covered
The infographic includes the following key aspects of digital literacy:

- Meaning of Digital Literacy  
- Safe Internet Practices  
- Professional Online Presence  
- Email Etiquette  

These topics were chosen to provide a basic understanding of how students can use digital platforms safely and effectively.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome
Through this task, I learned how to present important information in a clear and visually appealing way. I also understood the importance of digital awareness, safe online behavior, and maintaining a professional digital identity.
